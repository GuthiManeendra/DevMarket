# DevMarket brand style guide

## Brand idea

**DevMarket** is a curated digital marketplace for developer projects and professional portfolios. The brand should feel like a confident technology startup: precise, useful, premium, and built for people who ship.

**Tagline:** Discover. Create. Innovate.

## Logo concept

The DevMarket mark uses a compact rounded-square container with a code-bracket motif and a forward slash. The brackets communicate development and the slash communicates momentum, craft, and shipping. The blue-to-violet gradient makes the mark recognizable on dark surfaces while the dark container keeps it legible on light surfaces.

Use the SVG component at `src/assets/icons/DevMarketMark.jsx` for navigation and future favicon exports. Keep clear space equal to the width of one slash stroke. Do not recolor the mark arbitrarily, rotate it, or use emoji as a substitute.

## Voice

- Clear and confident, never overhyped.
- Helpful to beginners and credible to experienced developers.
- Use short action labels: “View details”, “Live demo”, and “Explore”.
- Avoid filler such as “revolutionary” unless the product evidence supports it.

## Visual direction

Use deep navy surfaces for premium moments, light backgrounds for discovery and forms, and the electric-blue-to-violet gradient for emphasis. Cyan is an accent, not a second primary color. Glows should be subtle and should never reduce text contrast.
